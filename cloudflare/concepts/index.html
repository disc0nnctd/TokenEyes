TokenEyes Concept Sprint

Seven sharper directions.

Added 4 more dark-first explorations based on your feedback. The explicit midpoint between `B` and `C` is `Concept D`, plus three random-but-promising variants.

Editorial Curiosity Pressable

Concept A: Price Is a Headline

An editorial front page. Strong for screenshot culture, newsletters, and people who share weird internet tools.

  • Magazine layout
  • Paper contrast and bold facts
  • Built for memorable first impression
Utility Confidence Tech

Concept B: Signal Terminal

A sharper AI-cost console. This treats TokenEyes like a serious money-to-compute instrument instead of a novelty toy.

  • Dark command-center tone
  • Premium data UI
  • Better fit for builders and AI power users
Consumer Viral Playful

Concept C: Social Bento

A brighter growth-oriented direction. Designed to feel like something people show friends, not just use once.

  • High color energy
  • Clear cards and reactions
  • Best if you want broader traffic
Hybrid B + C Balanced

Concept D: Hybrid Core

The direct midpoint of B and C. Keeps trust and utility, adds stronger consumer-facing visual energy.

  • Dark professional base
  • Brighter card accents
  • Best first candidate for production
Experimental Neon Niche

Concept E: Neon Deck

A sharper cyber aesthetic for a bold brand edge. Not for everyone, but highly distinctive.

  • HUD style interface
  • Monospace technical tone
  • Strong identity potential
Mature Structured Product

Concept F: Monolith

Hard-edged product design with bold hierarchy. Useful if you want "serious tool" perception fast.

  • Strong type-led look
  • Minimal visual noise
  • High trust positioning
Calm Dark Friendly

Concept G: Night Shift

A softer premium dark mode: less terminal, more consumer-friendly while still looking high quality.

  • Balanced dark palette
  • Readable card rhythm
  • Low-risk redesign path
